ingredients
-----------
- chili flakes (can use harissa)
- garlic (2/3 cloves)
- 1 med/small onion
- capers (handful)
- anchovies (~6)
- 1 med tomato (or teaspoon of tomato paste)
- artichoke hearts (1 pack)
- pine nuts (~2 tablespoons)
- basil 
- olive oil
- pasta (~250g)

instructions
------------
0. toast pine nuts on med heat for a few minutes. wait until they brown but take them off heat before they blacken.
1. finely cut garlic and onion, sautee in pan with oil on medium heat with chili flakes + tomato (cook for 5-10min, until onions are soft and translucent)
2. add capers, anchovies, artichoke hearts to pan and cook until anchovies break down (really u just want to heat the ingredients and mix the flavors, dont need to cook this for long)
3. cook pasta and set aside ~100ml of starchy pasta water
4. combine pasta and sauce with some of the pasta water in bowl
5. add chopped basil and pine nuts, serve with s&p
