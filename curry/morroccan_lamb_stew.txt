spice rub:
2 tbsp cumin
1 tbsp coriander
1 tbsp chili powder 
1 tbsp thyme
salt + pepper

stew:
oil
~6 cloves of garlic
1/2 finely chopped carrots
2 large onions
chickpeas
potatoes
splash white wine
1 large can tomato

recipe:
1. rub meat, leave for ~30mins
2. brown meat in oil (cook for no more than a few mins). Then set aside and keep warm.
3. deglaze with a little wine
4. add veg, cook for a few to soften and flavor
5. add rest of spices to veg
6. add meat and tomates, cover and put in oven (low F for long time — depends on weight)
